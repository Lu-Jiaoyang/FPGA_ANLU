Both recordings were excerpted to 1.6 seconds, converted to mono / 16 kHz,
DC-corrected, softly limited, faded at the endpoints and quantized to signed
8-bit PCM. `manifest.json` records source and output SHA-256 hashes.
The audition WAVs reproduce the FPGA's 48 kHz sample hold and output gain.
